## Foundation models

DECONVersation supports cell embeddings from the following single-cell foundation models:

- **scGPT** — Cui, H., Wang, C., Maan, H., Pang, K., Luo, F., Duan, N. & Wang, B.
  scGPT: toward building a foundation model for single-cell multi-omics using
  generative AI. *Nature Methods* **21**, 1470–1480 (2024).
  [doi:10.1038/s41592-024-02201-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-024-02201-0)

- **Geneformer** — Theodoris, C. V., Xiao, L., Chopra, A., Chaffin, M. D.,
  Al Sayed, Z. R., Hill, M. C., Mantineo, H., Brydon, E. M., Zeng, Z., Liu, X. S.
  & Ellinor, P. T. Transfer learning enables predictions in network biology.
  *Nature* **618**, 616–624 (2023).
  [doi:10.1038/s41586-023-06139-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06139-9)

- **CellHermes** — Gao, Y., Wang, W., Zhao, Y., Dong, K., Shan, C., Zheng, W.,
  Richter, T., Li, Z., Chen, S., Theis, F. J. & Liu, Q. Language may be all omics
  needs: Harmonizing multimodal data for omics understanding with CellHermes.
  *bioRxiv* (2025), preprint.
  [doi:10.1101/2025.11.07.687322](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.11.07.687322)

- **Cell2Sentence** — Levine, D., Rizvi, S. A., Lévy, S., Pallikkavaliyaveetil, N.,
  Zhang, D., Chen, X., Ghadermarzi, S., Wu, R., Zheng, Z., Vrkic, I., Zhong, A.,
  Raskin, D., Han, I., de Oliveira Fonseca, A. H., Ortega Caro, J., Karbasi, A.,
  Dhodapkar, R. M. & van Dijk, D. Cell2Sentence: Teaching Large Language Models
  the Language of Biology. *Proceedings of the 41st International Conference on
  Machine Learning (ICML)*, PMLR **235**, 27299–27325 (2024).
  [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/levine24a.html) ·
  [doi:10.1101/2023.09.11.557287](https://doi.org/10.1101/2023.09.11.557287)
